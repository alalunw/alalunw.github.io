Welcome to **Thoughts In Tremolo** — a space for exploring ideas at the intersection of technology, culture, and human experience.

## What to Expect

This is a digital garden, not a traditional blog. Rather than chronological posts, think of this as a collection of evolving thoughts, projects, and essays. Some pieces here will be polished; others will be works in progress.

### Topics I'll Explore

- **Technology & Society**: How digital tools shape human behavior and culture
- **Design & Aesthetics**: The intersection of form and function in digital products
- **Personal Projects**: Experiments, prototypes, and creative coding
- **Notes & Observations**: Half-formed thoughts and fleeting observations

## Design Philosophy

The design of this space is intentionally minimal and refined. I believe that good design should:

1. **Disapear** — Content should take center stage
2. **Adapt** — Work seamlessly across devices and contexts
3. **Delight** — Small moments of interaction should feel considered

The typography uses [Playfair Display](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Playfair+Display) for headings (a distinctive serif with editorial qualities) paired with [Source Sans Pro](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Source+Sans+Pro) for body text (clean, readable, and versatile).
