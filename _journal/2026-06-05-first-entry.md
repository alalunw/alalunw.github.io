Today marks the beginning of my journal. I've decided to create a space for less structured thoughts, more like a diary. Unlike the Writing section which features reviews and opinion pieces, this journal is for personal stories and reflections.

I'm sitting here thinking about how the blog has evolved. It started as a place for music reviews and has grown into a digital garden of ideas. Now, I want to add a more intimate corner where I can share my day-to-day experiences without the pressure of crafting a polished essay.

The sky outside is a soft gray, hinting at rain. It's the kind of weather that makes me want to curl up with a book and a cup of tea. Instead, I'm here, typing away, trying to capture this moment.

This is just the first entry. I hope to fill these pages with honest reflections, small joys, and the occasional struggle. Let's see where this journey takes us.
